Colour, light first

Eight palettes for a white-first app with an optional dark mode. Each one is shown three ways: an onboarding screen, the app itself, and a small dark-mode version.

Mostly neutral, one accentAbout 90% warm whites and greys, one accent colour for emphasis, and black for primary buttons (like Dia's black pills).
Accent ≠ button textBright accents fail contrast with white text, so buttons stay ink. The accent goes on the italic word, the active sidebar item, the scan dot and the mascot.
Stack colours stay loudRust orange, TypeScript blue and so on come from each language. The app colour should be calm enough not to fight them.
Dark is derivedSame accent hue, slightly lighter; warm near-black instead of pure #000. It's designed second and should never look like a different app.
