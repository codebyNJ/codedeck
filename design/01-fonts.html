Choosing the type for Codedeck

Four directions, each shown where it matters most: the three-screen onboarding story, the wordmark, and an in-app card. Pick one, or mix two, and that sets the tone for the mascot and the brand deck.

RULE 1
Two voices, never three

A brand font for the story moments (onboarding, empty states, website, deck) and the native system font for everything you use daily. Mono only for paths and commands.

RULE 2
Spend the "novelty budget" once

Dia's rule: daily UI stays familiar; character goes only where it adds feeling. For us that's onboarding, the first scan, and the mascot. Cards and lists stay quiet.

RULE 3
Must be bundlable

All four options are under the SIL Open Font License, so we can ship them inside the .app and use them on the site and in the deck for free.

01

Editorial — Instrument Serif

My pick

Calm, warm and premium; the italic gives each story screen one emphasised word. It reads like a well-made Mac app rather than another dev tool, and a soft mascot sits comfortably next to it. Mono: Geist Mono.

02

Soft & friendly — Fraunces (Soft)

Best with a cute mascot

Fraunces with its "soft" axis turned all the way up, so the serifs are rounded and a little bouncy. The friendliest option, close to a storybook. The risk is that it tips into cute if the mascot is also cute. Mono: JetBrains Mono.

03

Characterful grotesk — Bricolage Grotesque

Most "indie"

A sans serif with quirks: ink traps and a squat, chunky feel at large sizes. It's modern and confident with some personality, and suits a bolder, graphic mascot. It has no italic, so emphasis is done with colour. Mono: Geist Mono.
