Fraunces, and its neighbours

You liked Fraunces, so the first block tunes Fraunces itself four ways. Below that are free fonts with the same warm, soft-serif feeling from Google Fonts and Fontshare, then partners for body text, then the curators worth browsing.

Type anything · wrap a word in *stars* to emphasise it

A · Fraunces, tuned

Fraunces has two extra dials. SOFT rounds the serifs, and WONK adds the leaning, quirky letters (the n, h and m tilt). Same font, very different personalities.

B · Same feeling, other fonts

Warm, characterful serifs. Fontshare fonts are drawn by Indian Type Foundry and are free for commercial use. Their licence is a little stricter than Google's on redistribution, so I'll check the fine print on bundling before we ship.

C · Partner for longer text (website, deck)

Inside the app, body text stays in the Mac system font. The website and deck need a body font next to Fraunces.

D · Curators worth browsing

Places where people pick and preview good free fonts. Send me any font you like from these and I'll add it to this page.

Google FontsOFL

The biggest open library. Filter by "feeling" (calm, playful, cute) and by variable axes.

 FontshareITF free

Indian Type Foundry's free collection. Quality close to paid fonts; Satoshi and Gambetta come from here.

 Uncut.wtfcurated

A hand-picked catalogue of contemporary free and libre typefaces. The most "design Twitter" taste of the lot.

 Collletttivoopen source

An Italian open-source type collective. Expressive display faces, good for a logo or wordmark.

 Velvetynelibre

A French libre foundry. Experimental and bold; the best source for an unusual wordmark.

 Open Foundrycurated

Open-source fonts shown as beautiful specimen pages, which makes judging them easy.

 Badass Libre Fonts by Womxnlibre

Libre fonts designed by women, many of them display faces with lots of character.

 Typewolfinspiration

Shows which fonts real sites use, plus pairing guides. Good for seeing a font "in the wild".

 Fonts In Usearchive

A searchable archive of real brands, packaging and apps, by typeface. Use it to see how far a font can stretch.
