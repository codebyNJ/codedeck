    What is Repobox?

    A Mac app that finds every code repo on your computer and lists the Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode conversations that belong to each one. Open the project in your editor, or resume any session in one click.

    How do I resume an old Claude Code session?

    Find it in Repobox, or press ⌘K and type part of its name, then press Resume. Repobox opens Terminal in that project's folder and runs claude --resume with the session's ID.

    Does it upload my code or chats?

    No. It only reads files that are already on your Mac. There's no account, no AI inside, and nothing is sent anywhere.

    What do I need?

    macOS 13 or later and at least one of Claude Code, OpenAI Codex CLI or OpenCode. Works with Terminal and iTerm.
